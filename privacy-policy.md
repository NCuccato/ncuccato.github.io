## Privacy Policy — G6PD Scanner
Last updated: March 2026
## 1. Overview
G6PD Scanner ("the app") is developed by NCuccato. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy.
## 2. Data we collect
G6PD Scanner does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with the developer or any third party.
All data generated by the app is stored locally on your device only:

Scan history (product names and barcodes you have scanned)
Custom ingredients you have added
App preferences and settings

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer.
## 3. Third-party services
The app uses the following third-party services:

Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) — when you scan a barcode, the app sends the barcode number to the Open Food Facts public API to retrieve product information. No personal data is sent. Open Food Facts privacy policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy
Ko-fi (ko-fi.com) — if you choose to support the developer, you will be redirected to Ko-fi's website. Ko-fi's privacy policy applies at that point.

## 4. Camera permission
The app requests access to your device camera solely to scan product barcodes. No images or video are stored or transmitted.
## 5. Internet permission
The app requires internet access solely to query the Open Food Facts database when scanning a product.
## 6. Data retention and deletion
All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all app data at any time from the app's Profile page using the "Reset all app data" function, or by uninstalling the app.
## 7. Children's privacy
This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
## 8. Medical disclaimer
G6PD Scanner is a support tool only and does not constitute medical advice. The developer accepts no responsibility for health consequences resulting from the use of this app.
## 9. Changes to this policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this document.
